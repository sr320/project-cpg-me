project-cpg-me

CpG O/E Atlas

Germline DNA methylation history across invertebrates, estimated as CpG observed/expected (CpG O/E) from public genome annotations. Genes with low CpG O/E have lost CpGs to methylation-driven mutation over evolutionary time. Following Dimond & Roberts 2015.

Methods

  • Data. RefSeq annotated genomes from the NCBI Datasets API. CDS is the longest isoform per gene; gene body is the full gene span including introns; upstream is 1 kb before the gene on its strand. Features under 300 bp or with more than 5 % ambiguous bases are excluded.
  • CpG O/E = (nCG / (nC × nG)) × L² / (L − 1). GpC O/E is computed the same way as a composition control.
  • Mixtures. One- and two-component Gaussian mixtures per feature. A distribution is called bimodal when BIC favours two components by more than 10 and Ashman's D (separation of the two modes) exceeds 2.
  • Function. eggNOG-mapper 2.1.13 (eggNOG 5.0.2) on one protein per gene; GO terms mapped to GO Slim (generic, plus response to stress, translation and signal transduction). Housekeeping vs inducible: one-sided Mann-Whitney test; genes in both classes excluded.
  • Validation. WGBS reads aligned with Bismark to the same assembly; gene methylation is the weighted level over CpGs with ≥ 5 reads, for genes with ≥ 5 such CpGs.
  • Code, per-species outputs and the full write-up: github.com/sr320/project-cpg-me (PILOT.md).
